# Alert

An inline block at `radius-lg`: a 16px icon, a title in 600, a body in `ink`, optional action at the right. Five tones on their wash and border tokens: `alert-info` (brand), `alert-done`, `alert-waiting`, `alert-blocked`, `alert-regulatory`.

Say the uncomfortable thing plainly: what happened, what it blocks, what to do. The regulatory alert is the only place the adverse event clock is announced in prose; the StatCard shows the countdown.

Alerts stay until the condition clears. For a transient confirmation use Notification.
