# ButtonGroup

Buttons that share a border. Three forms.

- **Attached group** (`btn-group`): secondary buttons with 1px overlap and the outer radius on the ends. `is-active` marks the chosen value with `brand-wash` and a `brand` border. Use for exclusive choices of two to five values (Day / Week / Month).
- **Segmented control** (`seg`): a pill track in `surface-3` with the active segment raised in `surface`. Use for a view switch that changes the whole screen, such as the marketing side / clinical side toggle in reports.
- **Split button**: a primary action plus an attached chevron that opens a dropdown of related actions (Publish / Publish and notify / Save as draft).

The consumer supplies the buttons; the group adds only borders and radii. Keep labels one or two words.
