# Dropdown

A 240px menu in `surface` at `radius-lg` with `shadow-md`, 6px inner padding. Items are 34px with a 16px icon and a 10px gap; section labels in 11px caps; shortcuts in mono at the right; `hr` between groups. The active item takes `brand-wash`; destructive items take `blocked` and sit last after a rule.

Opens below its trigger, 6px gap, flips above when there is no room. Escape closes; arrow keys move.
