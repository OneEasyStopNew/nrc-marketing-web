# Kanban

Columns in `surface-2` with a `line` border, 220px wide, scrolling horizontally; cards in `surface` at `radius-sm` with a code title in mono, one line of study and site, one line of the next thing with an icon and the owner avatar at the right.

The column header carries a dot in the stage's status colour and a count Badge. The column a card is being dragged over takes `brand-wash` and shows a dashed drop slot; the dragged card lifts with `shadow-md`.

Stages are the sixteen from eClinPro, in their order; a board shows a chosen subset (the five above are the usual recruiter view). Every card shows a code, never a name: the board is visible on both sides of the boundary.
