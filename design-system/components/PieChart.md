# PieChart

Always a donut (22px ring) with the total in the centre in `stat` mono and a caption under it. Segments in the chart series order, 2px `surface` gaps between them. The legend is a small table: swatch, label, count, percentage in mono. Four to six segments at most; fold the rest into "Other" in `chart-4`.

Use for the composition of a funnel at one moment; use a BarChart for change over time.
