# CampaignPacing

A DataTable row per campaign: code, study and sites, a ProgressBar of spend against the cap with the figures in mono under it, a pace Badge with the rate, enrolled and cost per enrolled in tabular numbers, the stop condition, and one action.

Tones: `done` on pace or stopped by rule, `waiting` behind, `blocked` over cap or paused by the platform. Enrollments come from the clinical side as a count only (the enrollment signal), never as who; the head says "Enrollments, not likes". "Paused by Meta" is shown plainly with an Appeal action, because Meta can pause a campaign without warning.
