# AIAssistant

A Card chat. The assistant avatar is the spark on `brand-dark`; the person's is their initials. Assistant turns sit on `surface-2`, cite the module and time they drew from in `caption-regular`, and may offer actions as secondary buttons; the person confirms every action. A tools row (copy, retry, helpful / not helpful) sits under the last answer.

The head badge scopes the assistant to one side and one site: it answers from that side's data only. It never publishes, signs, approves or crosses the boundary; a request to do so gets a plain refusal and a link to the right surface. Codes, never names, in answers shown on the marketing side.
