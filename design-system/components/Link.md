# Link

Links are `brand` text, no underline at rest, underline on hover with a 3px offset. Inside a sentence they look like the surrounding text in colour weight; they never shout.

- Icon links: 16px icon in `currentColor`, 6px gap, icon leads for a destination (globe, document) and trails for forward navigation (chevron).
- Muted links (`text-muted`) for secondary paths: "Skip for now", footer links at 13px.
- Destructive links take `blocked` and always say what they remove.
- A link goes somewhere; a button does something. Do not style a save as a link.
