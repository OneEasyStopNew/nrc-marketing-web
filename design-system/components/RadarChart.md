# RadarChart

Six axes on a hexagonal grid at thirds in `chart-grid`, labels in 11px `text-faint` outside the ring, polygons stroked at 2px and filled at 15% in the series colours. Two series at most; more than that becomes unreadable.

Use for comparing campaigns or sites on normalised scores (reach, pace, cost, quality, compliance, Spanish coverage). State the scale in the legend.
