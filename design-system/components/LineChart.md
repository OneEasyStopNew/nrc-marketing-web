# LineChart

2.5px lines with round joins; the primary series may carry a 12% area fill. A secondary series on a different scale is dashed so the two are not read on one axis. Hover draws a dotted `line-strong` rule, a 5px marker with a `surface` ring, and a dark tooltip with the period and values in mono.

Grid every 10 units in `chart-grid`; months on the x axis; the y axis starts at zero. Twelve points at most on a card-sized chart.
