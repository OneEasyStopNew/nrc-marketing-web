# Calendar

Month grid of 7 columns on `line-hair`, weekday headers in `caption`, today as a 20px `brand` disc. Events are 16px chips in the status washes: `brand-wash` scheduled, `done-wash` completed, `waiting-wash` waiting, `regulatory-wash` for AE reviews and audits. More than three events in a day collapse to "+n".

Toolbar: previous / Today / next group, the month title, and a Day / Week / Month segmented control. The mini calendar (240px) is the date picker: dots under days that have events, the chosen day in `brand`.

Events show times and visit codes, never participant names on the marketing side.
