# SignIn

A 760px card split into a 300px `brand-dark` slab (mark, product name, one sentence, the PHI-region line at the foot) and the form. Email, password with a "Forgot?" link in the label row, a keep-me-signed-in checkbox, a 44px primary button, then an "or" rule and an SSO secondary button.

Errors appear as an `alert-blocked` above the fields: "Email or password is incorrect" (never which). After five failures the form shows a wait message with the time. On a phone the slab becomes a 120px band above the form.
