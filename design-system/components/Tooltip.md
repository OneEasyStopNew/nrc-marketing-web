# Tooltip

`tooltip` ground (ink in light, `line` navy in dark), 12px text, 6px by 10px padding, `radius-md`, a 8px arrow. Appears after 300ms on hover and immediately on focus; disappears on leave or Escape. Max width 240px, wrapping.

Tooltips name icon-only buttons and explain a disabled control. They never hold the only copy of important information and never hold links.
