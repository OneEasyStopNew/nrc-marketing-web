# Carousel

A horizontal strip of slides with secondary round-cornered arrow buttons overlapping the edges, a dot indicator (the active dot stretches to 18px in `brand`) and a mono counter. Slides are `radius-lg` and show one creative, screenshot or document page each.

Use it to flip through a creative's placements (feed, story, reel) or an invoice's pages. Never autoplay. Arrows are keyboard focusable; the strip is also swipeable.
