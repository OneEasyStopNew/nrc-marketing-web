# LeadFunnel

Horizontal bars per stage, 16px tall at `radius-sm`, labelled left and counted right in mono, with the two conversion rates under the chart. Colours follow the status family of the stage, not a gradient.

The sixteen stages, in eClinPro's order, and their families: not contacted (neutral); contact attempted 1, 2, 3; pending follow-up; appointment confirmation required; scheduled for phone call; unresponsive (waiting); qualified; scheduled pre-screening; scheduled screening (brand); enrolled (done); not show; DNQ; pre-screening fail; not interested (blocked). The default funnel view collapses the attempts into one row and hides the blocked exits; "All 16 stages" expands.

Counts only. The funnel is visible on the marketing side because it carries no identity.
