# BarChart

Bars at 22px with a 3px top radius, grouped with a 4px gap, on `chart-grid` lines; ticks in 11px `text-faint`. Series colours in order: `chart-1` brand blue, `chart-2` teal, `chart-3` amber, `chart-4` slate; `chart-5` violet appears only in regulatory views. A target is a 2px dashed line in `waiting`.

Legend above right, 10px swatches. Title in 600 at the left. Numbers on hover via Tooltip. Empty months render in `chart-muted`. Two series beside each other differ in lightness, not only hue.
