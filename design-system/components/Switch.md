# Switch

36×20 track, `line-strong` off, `brand` on, 16px white knob. The regulatory switch (`is-regulatory`) turns `regulatory` when on and is used only for gates: "Require IRB number to publish", "Lock record after signature".

A switch applies immediately and needs no Save. Label on the right in `body`; in a settings list the label sits left with the switch at the row end. A locked policy renders the switch on at 50% opacity with "Locked by policy" under the label; it is still read by screen readers as disabled.
