# Inbox

Two panes: a conversation list (channel avatar in `av-sq`, title, last line, a state Badge: the 24-hour reply window countdown in `waiting`, "Names a condition" in `blocked`, "Handed to clinical side" in `done`) and the thread with quick-reply chips and a composer.

Rules: the composer placeholder says it: no health questions are answered here. "Hand to clinical" moves the conversation across the boundary and ends it on this side. Comments that name a condition can be hidden (not deleted) and are logged. Handles are hidden behind a lead code. Instagram and Messenger threads show the 24-hour window because Meta closes it.
