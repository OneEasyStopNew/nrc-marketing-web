# CreativeCard

250px Card: a preview stage with a Ribbon for the live or gated state and the placement in mono; a row with the IRB badge (`badge-regulatory`, or `badge-waiting` "No IRB number"), language and platform pills; the primary text at 13px with any failing phrase marked in `blocked`; the compliance and rights badges; a foot with metrics or the fix and gated publish buttons.

Publish stays a disabled `btn-regulatory` while any badge is not `done`. The empty card is the dashed drop zone with accepted formats and a "Generate draft" entry into AIImageGenerator. The failing example above is the one from the demo ("we pay you $1,200"): the card is specified around catching exactly that.
