# Modal

480 to 560px over `overlay`, `radius-xl`, `shadow-lg`. Head: optional icon, title in `heading-sm`, close at right. Body 20px padding. Foot on `surface-2` with Cancel (ghost) then the primary.

A regulatory dialog (IRB gate, Part 11 signature, adverse event acknowledgement) carries the shield in the head, a `alert-regulatory` block, regulatory focus rings and a `btn-regulatory` confirm. The gate cannot be bypassed from the dialog; the confirm stays disabled until the number validates.

One modal at a time. Escape and the scrim close a normal modal; a regulatory one closes only by Cancel.
