# Tabs

Underline tabs: 14px 500, 10px by 12px padding, 2px `brand` underline on the active tab, `text-muted` at rest. A count badge after the label (neutral at rest, `brand` when it needs attention). The Audit tab carries the `regulatory` shield.

Pill tabs (`tabs-pill`) for a platform or language switch inside a card, active in `brand-wash`.

A tab the role cannot open is shown at 50% opacity with a Tooltip saying why ("Needs finance access on eClinPro"), so the person knows whom to ask. Six tabs at most; beyond that, use a Sidebar section.
