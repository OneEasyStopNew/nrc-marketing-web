# VectorMap

A schematic region shape in `chart-muted` on a `line-strong` outline, with site markers: a 5px `brand` dot ringed in `surface` and a halo sized to the count behind it. Other centers show as 3px `text-faint` dots. Always label "Schematic, not to scale" in the corner, and pair the map with a List of the same figures so nothing depends on reading the map.

The Florida outline here is a simplified silhouette drawn for the preview, not survey geometry; replace it with a licensed region path when the real map ships. No basemap tiles on the marketing side.
