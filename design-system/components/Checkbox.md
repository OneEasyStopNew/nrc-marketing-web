# Checkbox

16px box, `radius-sm`, `line-strong` border. On: `brand` fill with a white check. Mixed (`is-mixed`): `brand` fill with a 8×2 bar; use only on a parent that controls children. Disabled: 50% opacity.

Label on the right, 8px gap, in `body`. A second line in `caption-regular` may explain the consequence.

A checkbox is a setting or a selection. A yes/no decision that takes effect immediately is a Switch.
