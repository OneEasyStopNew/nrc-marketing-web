# Sidebar

240px on `surface-2`, collapsing to a 56px icon rail. Items are 36px, `radius-md`, 20px icon with a 10px gap, `text-muted` at rest, `brand-wash` and `brand-strong` when active. Section labels in 11px caps.

Sections follow the order a participant meets the system: Patient front door (Volunteers, Front desk and visits, Messages), Studio (Campaigns, Creative library, Compliance), Operations (Reports, Study finance, Clinical follow-up, Administration). Compliance is the one item with a coloured icon, `regulatory`, with a regulatory count badge.

Items a role cannot use are absent, not disabled. The rail shows a tooltip on hover with the full label.
