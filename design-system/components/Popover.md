# Popover

A 280px card at `radius-lg` with `shadow-md` and a 10px arrow pointing at its trigger. Title in 600, body in 13px `text-muted`, an optional action row. Three uses: explain something (why a slot is unavailable), confirm something small inline (pause a campaign; a destructive confirm uses `btn-danger`), or show a figure on hover (campaign pace).

Anything that needs a form or more than two actions is a Modal, not a popover.
